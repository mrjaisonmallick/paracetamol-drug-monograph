Dosage and administration

Educational only. Doses below are summaries of the cited labels. They are not a substitute for an individual prescription. Count paracetamol from every source, including combination products.
A label maximum is a ceiling, not a target. 4 g per 24 h is the cap for adults and adolescents of 50 kg or more. Lower caps apply under 50 kg (75 mg/kg, up to 3,750 mg) and in infants and neonates (60 and 50 mg/kg). The labels also advise caution, and a lower daily dose may be warranted, in hepatic or severe renal impairment, alcoholism and malnutrition. {1,5}
